## Candidate decisions and edit evidence

Choose tools, object boundaries and processing order from the source image and the user's editing goal. Preserve wording, numbers and complete objects. Pixel changes require proven ownership. Keep unsupported regions in their original raster backing and report which edits remain unavailable.

Read the current candidate IDs, validation results and scene version before starting another reconstruction. Use a compatible VALIDATED candidate for the requested edit and commit it with its ID and current version. After a version conflict, refresh the scene and candidate state. Try a different route when a concrete defect or new evidence justifies it. Record the affected object and the reason. Reuse confirmed work when the inputs, effective settings and relevant scene evidence still match.

Inspect real edit evidence from a saved and reopened copy. For an independently movable icon, move the whole icon clear of its old footprint. Compare the whole original site and its immediate surroundings before and after. For separately editable foreground and backing, inspect the foreground removed on its own, then both layers removed together. Use independent backing evidence when available. A wider inspection area grants no additional pixel ownership. Record untested actions, ambiguous outlines and missing evidence as unverified.

Judge replacement text in the rendered page at reading size. Preserve the wording, digits, punctuation, hierarchy and ability to edit the paragraph. A different typeface, weight or natural line break is acceptable when the result reads clearly and fits its surroundings. If the ink crosses its old frame, inspect the cleared background and neighboring content. Missing characters, visible clipping, collisions and stale text require local correction. Stop searching fonts once the result works.

Finish when the requested edits have adequate evidence or further work lacks a safe, worthwhile route within budget. Report acceptable deviations, verified edits and raster areas. A validated candidate still requires submission, compilation and the configured final checks before delivery.
